# Sonar Duo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 26, 2026_

Sonar Duo is a game for iPhone, made for iPhone Duo. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Sonar Duo does not collect, store or share any personal data with us or with anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers.

## Your captain name

You can type a captain name in the lobby. It's stored only on your device. When you search for nearby players, your name is shown to other Sonar Duo players near you, and it's sent directly to the one player you connect with. It never reaches us.

## Nearby play

Two-player games connect directly between two nearby devices using Apple's MultipeerConnectivity (Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi). That connection is encrypted by iOS. Only game moves are exchanged: where a torpedo was fired and whether it hit. Your fleet's positions stay on your device until a submarine is sunk.

## Permissions

- **Local network:** to find nearby players on your Wi-Fi network.
- **Bluetooth:** to connect directly to a nearby player.

You can turn these off in Settings at any time. Play vs Computer works without them.

## Children

Sonar Duo collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
